Our Story

Pioneering Pure Water Since 2011

Duro Water Technologies was founded with a singular mission: to make crystal-clear, safe drinking water accessible to every home and business. What began as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into a leading enterprise trusted by over 5,000 customers and 250+ corporate clients.

We specialize in reverse osmosis, UV, UF, and alkaline purification technologies — delivering solutions that range from compact kitchen purifiers to large-scale industrial RO plants. Every system is engineered for reliability, efficiency, and long-term performance.
